Sound Manipulations in INTEGRATED.py

General Approach:
- Each visual triggers a sound file from the 'sounds' directory when people are detected.
- Sound properties (pitch, volume, equalizer) are adjusted based on the movement of tracked keypoints.
- Visuals tracking multiple keypoints (e.g., ElbowTrailsVisual) have separate manipulations for each keypoint per person, ensuring distinct audio feedback.
- Visuals tracking a single keypoint (e.g., AccelerationGlowVisual) use one manipulation per person.

Specific Manipulations:

1. AccelerationGlowVisual
   - Tracked Keypoint: Left wrist
   - Sound File: acceleration_glow.wav
   - Manipulations:
     - Pitch: Adjusted based on wrist speed (higher pitch for faster movement, range 0.5 to 2.0).
     - Volume: Increases with the number of people detected (base 0.5 + 0.1 per person).
   - Multi-Person: Each person has one manipulation based on their left wrist's position and speed.

2. ElbowTrailsVisual
   - Tracked Keypoints: Left elbow, right elbow
   - Sound File: elbow_trails.wav
   - Manipulations:
     - Left Elbow:
       - Pitch: Shifted based on speed (base 1.0 + speed/100, range 0.5 to 2.0).
       - Volume: Adjusted by speed (base 0.5 + speed/100, max 1.0).
     - Right Elbow:
       - Pitch: Shifted differently based on speed (base 1.0 + speed/50, range 0.5 to 2.0).
       - Volume: Adjusted by speed (base 0.5 + speed/100, max 1.0).
   - Multi-Person: Each person's left and right elbows have separate pitch shifts and volume adjustments.

Activation:
- Within the 'run_visualization' function:
  - Person count and trails are updated via user_data.detections in the app_callback.
  - For each person and keypoint, a unique audio pipeline is created with a key like "person_X_keypoint_soundfile".
  - Sound properties are adjusted using GStreamer elements:
    - Pitch: pitch.set_property("pitch", value).
    - Volume: volume.set_property("volume", value).
  - Multi-keypoint visuals (e.g., ElbowTrailsVisual) apply distinct manipulations per keypoint per person.

Notes:
- Sound files are in the 'sounds' directory, named after visuals (e.g., elbow_trails.wav).
- All sound files should be normalized to -23 LUFS for consistent loudness.
- Multiple GStreamer pipelines handle distinct sound manipulations for different keypoints and people.
